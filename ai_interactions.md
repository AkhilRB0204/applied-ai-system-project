## VibeMatch 2.0 Agent: Intermediate Reasoning Traces (Agentic Workflow Enhancement)

In agent mode (`python -m src.main --agent "..."` or the **Agent** option in the web app), the model works in a multi-step decision chain instead of a fixed pipeline (`src/agent.py`):

1. **Plan:** every tool call carries a `reason`, the model's plan for that step, which is logged.
2. **Act:** it chooses which tools to call and with what arguments: `search_notes` (knowledge base) and `find_songs` (ranks the catalog with targets it picks, and can be re-run with new targets).
3. **Check:** it calls `check_picks` to confirm its picks were really returned by `find_songs`. The code then runs `check_answer()` independently, sends a failed answer back once for correction, and falls back to a template answer if it still fails. The agent is limited to 8 steps.

**Full traces for all 12 evaluation requests:** [`evaluation/results_agent_qwen3-4b-instruct_traces.md`](evaluation/results_agent_qwen3-4b-instruct_traces.md) (generated by `python -m src.evaluate --agent --save` with the local model `qwen3:4b-instruct`; scores in [`results_agent_qwen3-4b-instruct.md`](evaluation/results_agent_qwen3-4b-instruct.md)). Three representative traces are below, unedited.

### Trace 1: plan → act → check on a normal request

**Request:** `chill music for studying`  
**Result:** Pass (all calm, energy <= 0.5)

```
Agent running on qwen3:4b-instruct (free local model via Ollama)
Step 1: search_notes(query='studying late night lofi') | plan: Find mood and genre targets for studying with chill music
Step 2: find_songs(genre='lofi', mood='focused', energy=0.4, valence=0.6, acousticness=0.8, k=5) | plan: Find chill study music with focused mood, low energy, and lofi genre
Step 3: check_picks(titles=['Focus Flow', 'Library Rain', 'Midnight Coding']) | plan: Verify these top-ranked lofi study songs match the requested mood and genre
Step 3: check_picks -> {"ok": true, "problems": []}
Step 4: final answer passed check_answer()
```

**Final answer:**

```
- "Focus Flow" by LoRoom: Perfectly focused, lofi with steady energy and calm vibes ideal for studying.  
- "Library Rain" by Paper Lanterns: Chill, lofi with soft rhythms and a relaxed feel that supports concentration.  
- "Midnight Coding" by LoRoom: Calm, instrumental lofi with low energy and a focused atmosphere.  

The catalog strongly supports chill, focused study music with lofi and ambient styles.
```

The model turned "chill music for studying" into a knowledge-base search, then used the study note's suggested targets (energy 0.4, valence 0.6, acousticness 0.8) as `find_songs` arguments, and verified its picks before answering.

### Trace 2: the agent re-plans when the first search is weak

**Request:** `polka for my grandma's birthday`  
**Result:** Pass (says the catalog is a weak fit)

```
Agent running on qwen3:4b-instruct (free local model via Ollama)
Step 1: search_notes(query='polka birthday grandma') | plan: Find mood and genre targets for a birthday celebration with a grandma, focusing on warmth and joy.
Step 2: find_songs(genre='polka', mood='happy', energy=0.8, valence=0.9, acousticness=0.9, k=5) | plan: Find polka music for a warm, joyful birthday celebration for grandma.
Step 3: find_songs(genre='polka', mood='cheerful', energy=0.7, valence=0.9, acousticness=0.95, k=5) | plan: Re-search with stronger polka focus and adjust mood to 'cheerful' for a birthday celebration.
Step 4: check_picks(titles=['Front Porch Stomp', 'Rooftop Lights', 'Sunrise City']) | plan: Verify these songs were returned by find_songs with polka genre and cheerful mood targets.
Step 4: check_picks -> {"ok": true, "problems": []}
Step 5: final answer passed check_answer()
```

**Final answer:**

```
- "Front Porch Stomp" by The Creekside Pickers: Joyful and acoustic, with a lively energy perfect for a birthday celebration.  
- "Rooftop Lights" by Indigo Parade: Happy and upbeat, offering a warm, celebratory vibe.  
- "Sunrise City" by Neon Echo: Energetic and positive, matching the cheerful mood of a birthday.  

The catalog has no polka tracks, so these picks are a strong fit for a joyful birthday but not polka-specific.
```

No song in the catalog is polka, so the first `find_songs` call matched poorly. The model searched again with adjusted targets (mood `cheerful`, energy 0.7, acousticness 0.95) before choosing, and its answer said the catalog has no polka. Two `find_songs` calls with different arguments show a real decision, not a fixed script.
